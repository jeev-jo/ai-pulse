## What is AI Pulse?

AI moves fast. Most coverage is either too technical, too shallow, or buried in hype.

**AI Pulse** is a weekly article published every **Tuesday** that takes the most important AI developments from the past seven days and explains them in plain language — with context, citations, and an honest take on what it means for real people.

No panic. No hype. Just the signal.

---

## 🧭 What to Expect in Each Issue

Every Tuesday article covers:

- **The biggest AI development** — what happened and why it matters
- **Market & economic impact** — how it's affecting industries, stocks, and jobs
- **Plain-language explanation** — no jargon, no assumed knowledge
- **Cited sources** — every claim linked to its origin
- **What you can do** — practical, honest suggestions
